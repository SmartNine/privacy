Privacy Policy

MZ Game Studio · Effective date: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the following games published by MZ Game Studio on Google Play (together, the "Games"):

  • Moto Outlaw: Combat Racing (com.mzgame.car.moto)
  • Starship Blitz (com.mzgame.skyracing)

Summary

The Games are single-player offline games. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. The Games contain no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking, and they do not require an account.

Information we collect

None. The Games do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, device identifiers, photos or any other personal data, and they do not send any data to our servers (we do not operate any servers for the Games).

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (for example gold, unlocked vehicles, settings and completed stages) is saved only on your device. This data never leaves your device, and it is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its app data.

In-app purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment details. Google provides us only with the information needed to confirm a purchase (such as an order number and the item purchased). Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

The Games request only the permissions needed to run: vibration for haptic feedback, and the network and billing permissions required by Google Play to process in-app purchases. The Games do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, location, phone or storage.

Children

The Games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at mzgamecenter@gmail.com.
